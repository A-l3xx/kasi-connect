# Kasi Connect

## Overview

Kasi Connect is a township-focused digital marketplace designed to connect local customers with small businesses, informal traders, entrepreneurs, and service providers within South African communities. The platform provides a digital space where local businesses can showcase their products and services, while customers can discover, browse, and purchase from trusted local sellers.

The primary goal of Kasi Connect is to promote local economic growth, increase digital inclusion, and help township businesses establish an online presence. The platform bridges the gap between local consumers and community businesses by providing an easy-to-use, mobile-friendly marketplace that supports both products and services.

---

# Project Objectives

The objectives of Kasi Connect are:

- Support township entrepreneurs and local businesses.
- Increase visibility of small businesses and service providers.
- Create a digital marketplace tailored to township communities.
- Provide customers with convenient access to local products and services.
- Promote local economic development.
- Enable digital transformation for informal and small businesses.
- Create opportunities for future expansion into payments, delivery services, and business analytics.
